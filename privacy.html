Privacy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page describes the data handling visible in the HAZIK website source. The site owner should keep it accurate as hosting, project data, or third-party services change.

Information and storage

The site stores your selected display theme in your browser's local storage. The project catalog is requested from the site's Supabase backend. The upload page sends project details to that backend when an authorized site administrator submits or edits a project.

The hosting provider and backend provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as request data and logs. Check the providers' current privacy documentation for details about their processing and retention.

Third-party services and links

Project pages may embed videos from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain and load fonts from Google Fonts. Project download links and the GitHub link take you to other websites, which have their own privacy practices.

This fixed version does not include advertising scripts. If advertising is added later, this policy must be updated to identify the ad providers and explain their use of cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies, as applicable. Google also requires publishers to disclose third-party ad cookies and provide the relevant privacy choices.

Your choices

You can clear local storage in your browser settings to remove the saved theme preference. You can choose whether to follow external links or play embedded videos.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through the HAZIK GitHub profile.
